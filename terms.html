Currency Converter

The Currency Converter uses official exchange-rate data published by the National Bank of Ukraine (NBU) through its Open Data service.

The App retrieves the public NBU exchange-rate table and performs the selected currency conversion locally on the user's device.

The monetary amount entered by the user and the selected currency pair are not included in the NBU exchange-rate request.

Exchange-rate information may be cached locally and may therefore not always represent the latest available market rate.

Currency conversions are provided for informational and general calculation purposes only. Exchange rates shown by the App should not be treated as binding quotations for:
  • Banking transactions
  • Foreign-exchange trading
  • Investment decisions
  • Payments
  • Accounting
  • Taxation
  • Commercial settlements
  • Other financial transactions
The National Bank of Ukraine provides the underlying public exchange-rate data but does not sponsor, endorse, certify, or operate Scientific Calculator.
